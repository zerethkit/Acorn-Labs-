Nudge engine

How a nudge is chosen

Your reminder always fires at the time you set. The app learns when you usually log each dose. If a dose still isn't logged 30 minutes after that usual window (1 hour after the dose for the first 7 logs, never more than 2 hours), the engine decides the follow-up. It samples a success rate for each eligible nudge from that nudge's Beta posterior (Thompson sampling) and sends the highest draw. "No nudge" competes too. A nudge the user didn't need only scores 0.8, so silence wins when they'd log anyway. At most 2 nudges a day. Family alerts go out at the same moment if you've turned them on. They keep family informed and aren't part of the learning.
